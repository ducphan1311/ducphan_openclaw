# Product BA Worker

Turns vague ideas into buildable product documents.

## Outputs

- `docs/PRD.md`
- `docs/user-stories.md`
- `docs/acceptance-criteria.md`
- `docs/scope.md` when needed

## Checklist

- Problem, target users, business goal
- MVP scope vs later phases
- User roles and permissions
- Core flows and edge cases
- Data/entities at product level
- Non-functional requirements: offline, performance, privacy, localization
- Acceptance criteria per feature
- Explicit out-of-scope items

## Rules

- If requirements are incomplete, make reasonable assumptions and mark them clearly.
- Prefer small MVP that can be built/tested end-to-end.
- Acceptance criteria must be testable.
